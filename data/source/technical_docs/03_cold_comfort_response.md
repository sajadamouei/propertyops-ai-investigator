# Cold Comfort Investigation Guide

Synthetic internal technical guidance for the PropertyOps AI Investigator demo.

## Occupant reports

Multiple cold-comfort complaints occurring shortly after abnormal HVAC behavior are useful evidence that the technical event had a real occupant impact.

Complaint timing should be compared with supply-air temperature, zone temperature, fan operation, and the preceding equipment event.

## Ventilation-related cold complaints

Reports of cold air from ventilation may indicate that supply-air temperature is lower than expected while the fan remains active.

Confirm the actual supply-air and zone temperatures before concluding that the ventilation system caused the complaints.

## Correlating evidence

A stronger investigation combines several independent signals: telemetry, occupant complaints, maintenance history, and technical guidance.

Complaints should support the investigation but should not be treated as proof of a specific mechanical failure.

## Recommended response

Confirm affected zones, validate temperature measurements, review HVAC behavior preceding the complaints, and prioritize inspection when multiple occupants report the same condition.
